# Acceptable Use Policy (including AI tools)

> Sample policy shipped with GRC-pal. Tailor it to your organization before publishing.

## Purpose
Set clear expectations for using company systems, data and AI tools safely.

## Scope
Everyone who uses company devices, accounts, networks or data.

## Policy
1. Use company systems for business purposes; limited personal use must not affect security or work.
2. Keep credentials private. Never share accounts, passwords or MFA codes.
3. Lock your screen when away and report lost or stolen devices to IT immediately.
4. Store company data only in approved systems. Do not copy customer data to personal devices or accounts.
5. Install software only from approved sources.
6. **AI tools:** use only AI services approved by security. Do not enter customer data, personal data,
   credentials or confidential source code into AI tools that are not approved for that data class.
   Review AI output before relying on it; you remain accountable for the result.
7. Report suspected phishing, malware or data exposure to the security team right away.

## Enforcement
Violations are handled through the HR disciplinary process.
